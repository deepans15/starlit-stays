# Guest-room wallpaper patterns
Ten choices: Plain, Ribbon Stripes, Pearl Dots, Lattice Diamonds, Willow Sprigs, Soft Arches, Starlight, Petal Fans, Rounded Brick and Cottage Panels.
Nine original transparent 64 x 64 SVG tiles in walls/ repeat at 32 x 32 game pixels over any of the existing eight wall colours. Plain uses no tile. All choices are included after the existing wallpaper purchase, with no extra decor bonus or charge. Existing rooms default to stripes.
The guest-room panel shows colour and pattern separately. Patterns remain with stored rooms, saves and shared hotel snapshots. Amenity/lobby interiors keep their illustrated room styles.
walls.js supplies STARLIT_WALL_PATTERNS. Every tile is registered in manifest.js. preview.html includes a tinted wall swatch gallery.

# Gym workouts
All twelve guests and the inspector have eight-frame running and dumbbell-curl loops. Running plays at 10 fps, curls at 4 fps. Feet and body anchors match the existing gym slots; only limbs move. Exercise frames load with core art rather than the deferred weather batch. Classic, Gold and Pink gyms use the same equipment anchors. No guest arrival, departure or table-placement mechanics were changed.
